Soil & receipt

One palette across the marketing site and the dashboard. The rule the whole thing is built around: amber marks money and nothing else, so a price reads as its own channel and a bill can be scanned rather than parsed.

Chart series

The ramp steps by hue through leaf, teal, blue, lime, magenta and deliberately never reaches amber. A data series that borrowed the money colour would be read as a cost.
