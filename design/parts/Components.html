The one rule

Amber is the money channel. It appears on costs, balances, prices, and usage totals — and on nothing else, ever. Not on a warning, not on a highlighted row, not on a chart series. A reader who has scanned two screens learns that amber means a number that costs them something, and every bill after that is scannable rather than parsed.
